# T2MBot
Explore Purdue Engineering majors, curricula, and career paths during First-Year Engineering.

# What T2MBot Can Do
Explain Purdue engineering majors
Compare multiple engineering disciplines
Discuss coursework and plans of study
Explore career pathways and industries
Identify majors that may align with student interests
Highlight gaps in exposure to different disciplines
Suggest questions to investigate before selecting a major

Examples:

"What's the difference between Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering?"
"I enjoy ENGR 131 and physics. What majors should I explore?"
"What careers can Materials Engineering lead to?"
"I want to work in sustainability. Which engineering majors should I learn more about?"

# What T2MBot Cannot Do
Predict T2M admission outcomes
Estimate likelihood of acceptance into a major
Recommend a single "best" major
Replace academic advising
Interpret official university policy

Students should always verify important decisions using official Purdue resources and academic advisors.
